We've provisioned an app reviewer account for our test tenant so you can see how the app works. To authenticate, follow these steps:

1. Launch the app
2. Click the blue "Enable System Extension" button
3. Click "Open System Settings" in the dialog that appears
4. Turn ON the FirezoneNetworkExtension in the System Settings dialog that appears
5. Click the blue "Grant VPN Permission" button
6. Click "Allow" when prompted. Now the app is ready to use.
7. Open the App's menubar menu
8. Click "Sign in"
9. **IMPORTANT**: Enter "firezoneqa" as the Account ID (without the quotes -- this is your App Review test tenant), and click "Go to Sign in Page"
10. Select "JumpCloud (App Reviewer Sign in)" as the sign in method
11. Enter the credentials provided and continue
12. The app should now be signed in

You should see a list of resources you can now connect to. For example, try loading "ifconfig.net" into your browser and notice that the IP address displayed is that of the Firezone gateway, not your actual IP address. This indicates your traffic is being tunneled to the "ifconfig.net" resource.
